Products on the panel

Multiple lenders compete on every product. Availability depends on your lender panel.

Line of credit

Revolving access to funds. Draw when needed and pay for what is used.

Used for payroll timing, slow weeks and day-to-day working capital.

Term loan

A fixed amount repaid on a fixed schedule.

Used for equipment, new locations and planned growth.

Revenue-based loans

An advance repaid as a share of sales, so repayment slows when revenue does.

Used by businesses with card or platform revenue and uneven months.

Inventory financing

Funds stock and supplier orders ahead of the sales they produce.

Used by sellers and distributors before peak season.

Invoice financing

An advance on unpaid invoices, repaid when the customer pays.

Used by businesses that sell on 30 to 90 day terms.

Asset-based lending without field exams

Traditional asset-based lending carries fixed costs that only multi-million facilities can absorb. Vyant replaces them with platform data, which makes facilities from about $50K viable.

Questions lenders ask

Who makes the credit decision?

You do, against your own credit box and policies. Vyant matches applications to programs and ranks offers for the customer.

Does Vyant lend or hold loans?

No. Vyant takes no credit risk and holds no loans. Financing is provided by the lenders on the network.

Which products can we offer?

Lines of credit, term loans, revenue-based loans, inventory financing, invoice financing and asset-based facilities. You can join with one program and add more.

Do we need an engineering team?

No. Banks without API capacity join through the Underwriting Suite.

What do we receive with each application?

Platform sales and payout data, bank and accounting data, bureau and registry checks, and the financing need the customer selected.

How is Vyant paid?

Lenders pay fees tied to processed applications and funded financing. We share the schedule when we discuss your program.

Questions platforms ask

Do we take any credit risk?

No. Loans are made and held by the lenders on the panel. Neither your platform nor Vyant puts loans on its balance sheet.

We already have an embedded lender. Can we still use Vyant?

Talk to us. A common starting point is routing the applications your current lender declines or under-serves to the panel.

Who makes the credit decision?

Each lender, against its own credit box. Vyant matches and ranks; it does not approve or decline.

What data do you need from us?

For pre-qualified offers, the sales and payout data you already hold for each customer. With the hosted application, customers connect their own accounts and you share nothing up front.

Can we switch integration approaches later?

Yes. Hosted, SDK and API all sit on the same lender panel, so moving between them changes the experience, not the program.

How do we get paid?

A revenue share on each financing funded through your platform, including renewals.
